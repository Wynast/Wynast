The University of Guelph notes a commitment to accessibility, in regard to “fostering an educational, working, and living environment, where all University community members experience an authentic sense of inclusion and belonging,” with a commitment to among other values accessibility and inclusion.[1]

Two important facets of accessibility are availability and design.
